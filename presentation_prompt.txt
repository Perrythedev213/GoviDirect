SLIDE 5 — OUR SOLUTION

Title:
Our Solution — GoviDirect

Explain GoviDirect's value proposition: connecting farmers directly with consumers while maintaining trust, quality control and a sustainable revenue model.

Four key features:

Direct Farm Dispatch
A standard formula calculates transparent dispatch costs so customers and farmers can understand the delivery cost.

Only 4% Platform Revenue
GoviDirect retains 4% of the product transaction value as platform revenue, returning 96% of the product sale revenue to the farmer.

Verified and Reviewed
Farmers can apply for verification by submitting a sample of their produce to GoviDirect for quality evaluation. Customers can also view seller reviews and verification status.

Quality Accountability
Farmers agree to GoviDirect's rules and are expected to accurately represent the quality of their products. Misleading listings can result in platform penalties and, where legally applicable, further legal action.

SLIDE 7 — HOW GOVI DIRECT WORKS

Title:
How GoviDirect Works

Create a clean six-step flow:

	Farmer Creates a Listing:The farmer lists the produce, price, quantity and quality.

	Optional Verification: The farmer can submit a sample to GoviDirect for quality evaluation and receive a Verified status if approved.

	Customer Orders: Customers browse listings, view seller reviews and verification status, add products to their cart and proceed to checkout.

	Payment:The customer pays through the payment portal. GoviDirect receives the transaction and allocates 96% of the product sale revenue to the 	farmer.

	Direct Delivery: The farmer and customer agree on a suitable transport method. The farmer dispatches the produce directly to the customer.

	Review and Tip:The customer receives the produce and can rate the farmer. Customers can also provide a voluntary tip, with 100% of the tip going to 	the farmer.

Show the flow:
	Farmer → Listing → Verification → Customer Order → Payment → Delivery → Review

SLIDE 8 — THE DREAM FOR GOVI DIRECT

Title:
The Dream for GoviDirect

Present four long-term objectives.

Regional Warehouses
As sales volume increases, GoviDirect aims to establish regional warehouses with appropriate storage and maintenance.

	Produce from multiple nearby farmers could be transported in bulk to regional warehouses. This could create economies of scale by consolidating 	transportation and potentially reducing the average distribution cost per unit while placing produce closer to consumers.

	Support the Primary Sector: Improve the living standards and economic opportunities of people working in Sri Lanka's agricultural and primary 	industries.

	Fresh Food, Less Waste: Promote access to fresh, minimally processed produce while reducing food waste. Suitable surplus produce approaching the end 	of its marketable period could be donated to communities rather than discarded.
